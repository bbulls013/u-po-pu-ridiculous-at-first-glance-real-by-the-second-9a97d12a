## The entry

> We sell ad space on the foreheads of sleeping subway commuters, and brands pay per nap because a dozing stranger's forehead is the one billboard a packed rush-hour crowd cannot look away from.

One sentence, 192 characters.

## Why it works as a pitch

The idea sounds absurd on its face — selling advertising on a stranger's
forehead while they nap — yet the business logic lands on the second look.
The odd thing being sold is named plainly (forehead ad space on sleeping
commuters), the payer is named (brands, paying per nap), and the reason it
holds together is concrete: a packed rush-hour crowd is a captive audience
with nowhere else to look, so the dozing forehead is genuinely valuable
ad inventory. The sentence turns from a laugh into a nod in a single line,
which is exactly what the brief asks for.
